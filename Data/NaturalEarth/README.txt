Natural Earth 1:10m data (public domain, https://www.naturalearthdata.com), used by the Campaign Editor
for the "Political map" background: land, lakes, land borders between states, and the label point of each state.
Files: ne_10m_land.geojson, ne_10m_lakes.geojson, ne_10m_admin_0_boundary_lines_land.geojson,
country_labels.csv (name;lat;lon, from ne_10m_admin_0_countries LABEL_X/LABEL_Y).
They cover the whole world, so they work for any theater.
